# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
A CTO, a senior developer, or anyone deciding in about five minutes whether the person behind these
products can create real value with AI. They arrive from a link, skim on a laptop or a phone, and
click Visit on whatever looks real. They have seen a thousand AI demo pages and distrust claims.

## Product Purpose
Proof is a portfolio of shipped products, each shown working. The page answers one question:
has this person produced actual results, self-taught, on nights and weekends, or do they only talk
about AI? Success is a visitor who clicks through to a live product and finds it holds up.

Home, top to bottom: the First Light hero ("Welcome"), Skillproof, DATproof, Modelproof, and a small
"also shipped" line for rls-guard. A short Who am I page carries interests and contact links.

## Brand Personality
Calm, exact, confident. The products are the star; the page frames them and gets out of the way.
One big moment (the hero), then stillness. Evidence over adjectives: every number on the page is
traceable to a file, a filing, or a measured run.

## Anti-references
- AI landing-page grammar: bento stat grids, tag and chip rows, logo walls, testimonials,
  numbered section counters, "scroll to explore" nudges, typing effects, cursor followers,
  scroll-jacking, rounded card + shadow on everything.
- Purple/blue gradient heroes, near-black with one neon accent, gold on dark, cream + serif +
  terracotta.
- Buzzword copy: "AI-powered", "cutting-edge", "passionate", "leverage", "seamless".
- Filler stats and receipts that cannot be traced to a source.

## Design Principles
1. Show the product working. A window into the real product beats any sentence about it.
2. One receipt or none. A number appears only if it is a real, traceable time or cost saving.
3. Motion only where something real changes. The hero is the one peak; below it, only the
   DATproof grid moves (real data filling in), plus small spring feedback on touch.
4. Practice what you preach. Hand-built, no framework, fast on a phone, measured in the
   visitor's own browser.
5. Nothing gated on motion or script. Reduced motion or no JavaScript still shows every word and
   number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Lighthouse accessibility 100 on both pages. Full keyboard paths with visible focus.
`prefers-reduced-motion` gets a still, finished page (fades allowed, no travel). No-JS and
no-WebGL renders are designed fallbacks, not blank space. Light mode only.
